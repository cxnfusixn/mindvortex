## 07-motion.png

Animation should earn its place on a website.

We use motion to guide attention, explain an interaction and express a brand’s character. A small transition can make a change easier to follow; a larger sequence can give a project its own rhythm.

The content still needs to be easy to read and navigate. Consider loading time, reduced-motion preferences and whether the effect helps people do what they came to do.

Save this for your next website brief.
